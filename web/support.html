Common topics

Apple Health: Grant Got Motion access in the Health app under Sharing → Apps. Denied types simply appear unavailable in Got Motion.

Notifications: Enable push in Got Motion Settings → Notifications, then allow the iPhone system prompt. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings.

Account deletion: In the app, open Settings → Privacy (or Account) → Delete account.
